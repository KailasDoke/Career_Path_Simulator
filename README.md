## **🎓 Career Path Simulator**



\### From Class 10 to Career — Explore Paths, Compare Choices, Simulate the Future.



> \*\*A Class 10 student's future isn't one prediction. It's a set of possible paths.\*\*

> Career Path Simulator helps students and parents explore those paths, understand their academic requirements and financial implications, discover funding possibilities, and simulate how the future changes when circumstances change.



\---



\## 🚀 The Problem



After completing Class 10, students often face one of the most important decisions of their academic life:



\*\*"What should I do next?"\*\*



The decision is rarely based on marks alone.



A student's possible future depends on multiple factors:



\* 📚 Academic performance

\* 🧠 Aptitude and strengths

\* ❤️ Interests

\* 💰 Family budget

\* 🌍 Preferred location/country

\* 🎓 Admission requirements

\* 💸 Scholarships and funding

\* 🏦 Education financing

\* 🔄 Alternative options if the preferred path doesn't work



However, most existing career guidance systems simplify this into:



\*\*Marks → Career Recommendation\*\*



Real decisions are much more complex.



\### The real question is not:



> "Which career should I choose?"



\### It is:



> "What possible paths can take me from Class 10 to a career, what will each path require, what will it cost, and what happens if my circumstances change?"



\---



\# 💡 Our Solution



\## Career Path Simulator



Career Path Simulator is an \*\*AI-powered education and career decision-support platform\*\* that transforms career planning from a single recommendation into an \*\*interactive pathway exploration experience\*\*.



Instead of predicting one career, the platform generates multiple possible pathways and allows students to explore:



```text

##### Student Profile

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Academic + Aptitude + Interests

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Financial Situation + Location

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Multiple Education Pathways

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Programs + Institutions

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Cost + Admission Requirements

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Scholarships + Funding

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Education Financing

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### "What If?" Simulation

##### &#x20;     ↓

##### Decision Matrix

```



\### Our core philosophy



> \*\*We don't predict a student's career — we simulate the paths that can lead there.\*\*

\# 🎯 What We Built



The prototype focuses on the complete decision-making journey rather than building a collection of disconnected features.



\### 1. 👤 Student Profile



Students can create a profile containing:



\* Academic performance

\* Subjects and strengths

\* Interests

\* Aptitude indicators

\* Annual education budget

\* Preferred countries/locations

\* Career interests

\* Financial constraints



This profile becomes the foundation for pathway simulation.

\### 2. 🧠 Aptitude \& Interest Exploration



The platform considers different dimensions of a student's profile instead of relying only on academic marks.



Example dimensions:



\* Logical reasoning

\* Numerical ability

\* Verbal ability

\* Problem solving

\* Technology interest

\* Engineering interest

\* Healthcare interest

\* Business \& finance

\* Design

\* Research

\* Environment



The purpose is \*\*exploration\*\*, not psychological diagnosis or a guaranteed career prediction.

\# 💰 4. Financial-Aware Career Planning



A career path is not realistic if affordability is ignored.



The platform therefore considers:



\*\*Education Cost + Family Budget + Scholarships + Funding Gap + Financing\*\*



For every pathway, students can understand:



| Financial Factor               |   Example |

| ------------------------------ | --------: |

| Estimated education cost       | ₹9,00,000 |

| Family contribution            | ₹6,00,000 |

| Potential scholarship          | ₹1,50,000 |

| Remaining funding gap          | ₹1,50,000 |

| Possible financing requirement | ₹1,50,000 |



> Financial figures in the prototype are clearly treated as estimates/demo data where applicable.

\# 🎓 5. Scholarship \& Funding Discovery



The platform connects pathways with potentially relevant funding opportunities.



Students can see:



\* Eligibility conditions

\* Academic requirements

\* Income-related conditions

\* Applicable education level

\* Funding/benefit information

\* Required documents

\* Source information

\* Verification/update status



The system does \*\*not\*\* claim that a student is guaranteed to receive a scholarship.



Instead, it uses transparent language such as:



\* Potentially eligible

\* Eligibility requires verification

\* Missing information

\* Not eligible based on known criteria

\# 🔄 7. "What If?" Future Simulator



This is one of the core features of Career Path Simulator.



Students can ask:



\### What if my budget decreases?



```text

Current Budget

₹3,00,000 / year



&#x20;       ↓



Budget reduced to

₹2,00,000 / year



&#x20;       ↓



Pathway analysis updates



✓ Lower-cost options become more relevant

✓ Some international options require additional funding

✓ Scholarship requirements become more important

✓ Financing requirement changes

```



Other scenarios include:



\* What if I don't get my preferred program?

\* What if I cannot take an education loan?

\* What if I receive a scholarship?

\* What if my academic performance changes?

\* What if I want to study abroad?

\* What if my budget increases?

\* What if my preferred pathway becomes unavailable?



\### Why this matters



Real life does not follow a fixed plan.



\*\*Our system models that uncertainty instead of pretending the future is deterministic.\*\*

\# 📊 8. Decision Matrix



The platform does not simply say:



> 🏆 "This is your best career."



Instead, it gives students a transparent comparison.



| Factor             | Path A | Path B |    Path C |

| ------------------ | -----: | -----: | --------: |

| Academic alignment |   High | Medium |      High |

| Interest alignment |   High |   High |    Medium |

| Financial fit      | Medium |   High |       Low |

| Location fit       |   High | Medium |      High |

| Admission fit      |   High | Medium | Uncertain |

| Estimated cost     |     ₹₹ |      ₹ |       ₹₹₹ |

| Funding options    |   High | Medium |      High |

| Duration           |  4 yrs |  3 yrs |     5 yrs |

| Uncertainty        | Medium |    Low |      High |



This allows the \*\*student and parent to make the final decision\*\*.

\* How different pathways compare

\* Scholarship requirements

\* Education financing

\* Alternative routes



\### Important design principle



```text

Structured Verified Data

&#x20;         ↓

&#x20;     Pathway Engine

&#x20;         ↓

&#x20;    Scenario Engine

&#x20;         ↓

&#x20;   AI Explanation

```



The AI does not independently invent:



\* Tuition fees

\* Admission requirements

\* Scholarship eligibility

\* Loan rates

\* Institution information



This reduces hallucination risk and makes the system more trustworthy.

\---



\# 🔐 Responsible AI \& Decision Support



Career decisions can have long-term consequences.



Therefore, Career Path Simulator is designed as a \*\*decision-support system\*\*, not an autonomous decision-maker.



The system:



\* Does not guarantee admission.

\* Does not guarantee scholarships.

\* Does not guarantee employment.

\* Does not guarantee salary outcomes.

\* Does not diagnose aptitude or personality.

\* Does not tell students that one career is objectively correct.

\* Clearly separates facts, estimates and assumptions.

\* Exposes uncertainty where information is incomplete.

\* Allows the student and parent to make the final decision.
